Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

1. Controller

Florian S. · github.com/autopilotclaw/storypilot/issues

2. Data we process

  • TikTok account data of the operator: basic profile info (open ID, display name, avatar) and access tokens, used only to upload the operator's own videos as drafts.
  • Statistics of the operator's own videos (views, likes, comments, shares) to plan future episodes.
  • Comments under the operator's own videos are counted to determine which story option won. Usernames are not stored; comment text is deleted after counting, only the vote totals and anonymous quotes are kept.

3. Storage

Tokens and data are stored locally on the operator's computer. Video files are stored temporarily for upload and deleted after successful publishing. No data is sold or shared with third parties.

4. Retention

Tokens are kept until access is revoked. Raw comment data is deleted immediately after counting.

5. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability and objection. Contact the controller above. You may also complain to a data protection supervisory authority.

6. Revoking access

TikTok access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app under Settings → Security → Manage app permissions.
