StoryPilot

StoryPilot is a private production tool for one creator. It produces short interactive story videos ("you decide what happens next") and uploads them as drafts to the creator's own TikTok account. The creator reviews every draft in the TikTok app and publishes it manually.

StoryPilot is not offered to the public. Only the account owner connects their own TikTok account.

What StoryPilot does

  • Uploads finished videos of the creator's own series to their TikTok inbox as drafts (Content Posting API, video.upload).
  • Reads basic profile info of the connected account (user.info.basic) and the list of the creator's own videos with their public statistics (video.list) to plan the next episode.
